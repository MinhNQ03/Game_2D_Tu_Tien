AI DISCLOSURE
Every tile in this pack is generated by CSAF's own procedural drawing engine (pixel_forge), which is
AI-written software. No image model, no scraped art and no third-party dataset was used, and no
external image was traced or retouched: each tile is drawn pixel by pixel from code, inside a locked
palette. The store listing carries itch.io's Generative AI disclosure with Graphics marked YES, and
that tag is deliberate over-disclosure rather than a technicality — under-disclosing risks delisting,
and the buyer is entitled to decide for themselves.
